Executive Summary

Between May and July 2026, JC's Auto Repair Shop generated 253, then 244, then 235 total leads per month, a gradual decline of 7.1% over the full quarter. Leads coming from the Google Business Profile listing stayed comparatively steady, while leads coming from the website fell at a faster rate. The available data shows that GBP-generated lead volume has been relatively more stable than website-generated leads during this reporting period.

Important Trends

Each item below separates the observed fact from LocalVisi Digital's reading of it.

Overall lead trend

FactTotal leads fell every month, from 253 to 244 to 235, a 7.1% decline over the quarter.

InterpretationThis is a gradual, consistent slide rather than a single sharp drop.

GBP vs. website lead trend

FactGBP-attributed leads fell 2.5% over the quarter (203 to 198) while website-attributed leads fell 26.0% (50 to 37).

InterpretationThe decline is concentrated in the website channel, not evenly spread across both lead sources.

Website call-action trend

FactWebsite Click-to-Call fell every month (24, 14, 9) while Website Form Submissions were roughly flat to up (26, 29, 28).

InterpretationThe decline appears specific to the call action on the website rather than to website lead capture in general.

Search visibility trend

FactSearch Console impressions fell 19.2% from May to June (81,300 to 65,700), a clean calendar-month comparison, while clicks rose 12.2% in that same period (139 to 156).

InterpretationFewer searches showed the listing at all in June, even though the click-through rate on those searches held up.

Stability of GBP calls

FactGBP Calls stayed within a narrow band across all three months: 193, 194, 191, a net change of only -1.0%.

InterpretationOf all the lead channels tracked, GBP phone calls were the most stable this quarter.

Data Quality & Reporting Notes

Data Quality & Reporting Notes

These limitations affect what can and cannot be fairly compared in this report. None of them have been worked around or estimated.

  • 1July's Google Search Console data covers July 7–August 3, not July 1–31. Any comparison touching July's clicks, impressions, CTR, or average position is directional only, not a calendar-month comparison.
  • 2Month 2's website interaction screenshot (form submissions/click-to-call) does not show a visible date range, so its exact reporting period is unconfirmed.
  • 3Website Click-to-Map is only available for May (1). It does not appear in the June or July reports and cannot be trended.
  • 4Keyword-level ranking data was not provided. Only Search Console's site-wide Average Position is available, which is not the same as tracking individual keyword ranks.
  • 5GA4 (Google Analytics) data was not provided in any of the three source reports.
  • 6Review count and star rating data were not provided. Only links to review-reply documentation were included in the source files.

Recommended Actions

These are recommendations for investigation based on the observed patterns, not claims about the underlying cause. A cause has not been established from this data alone.

  • 1Investigate the decline in website Click-to-Call (24 → 14 → 9). This is the steepest trend in the report and warrants a direct look at the call button's placement and behavior.
  • 2Investigate why website-generated leads declined (50 → 37 over the quarter), separate from the GBP channel, which held up better.
  • 3Review website conversion elements and the mobile click-to-call experience, given that call actions fell faster than form submissions.
  • 4Investigate the May→June decline in Search Console impressions (81,300 → 65,700), a clean, fully comparable drop in search visibility.
  • 5Obtain keyword-level rank tracking for future reports, so ranking movement can be shown beyond the site-wide average position.
  • 6Add GA4 or other conversion data in future reporting, to connect website actions to on-site behavior and conversions.
  • 7Add GBP visibility metrics (profile views, search views, map views) in future reports, to distinguish visibility trends from lead-conversion trends.
